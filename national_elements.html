National Flag

The National Flag is a horizontal tricolour of the Indian saffron(kesaria) at the top, white in the middle and Indian green at the bottom in equal proprtion.

National Anthem

The song Jana-gana-mana,composed originally in Bangla by Rabindranath Tagore,was adopted in its Hindi version by the Constituent Assembly as the National Anthem of India on January 24,1950.

National Song

The Song Vande-Mataram, composed in Sanskrit by Bakimchandra Chatterji, was a source of inspiration to the people in their struggle for Freedom. It has an equal Status with Jana-gana-mana.

National Bird

The Indian peacock, Pavo cristatus, the national bird of India, is a, colourful,swan-sized bird, with a fan-shaped crest of feathers, a white patch under the eye and the long, slender neck.
